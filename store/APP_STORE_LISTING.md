## Description
Structure Radar is Free #2: an educational scanner for USDT market structure on mid/small-cap pairs.

It labels Structure Shift, MA Regime, and Levels (support/resistance, triangles). Hits are heuristics for learning — not buy/sell calls.

What you get
• Scan across Binance, Bybit, Gate.io (full venues on Android)
• Timeframes 15m · 30m · 1H · 4H · 1D
• Chart detail with timeframe switch
• Glossary + a hard legal gate
• Languages: English, Español, Português, Русский — before the disclaimer
